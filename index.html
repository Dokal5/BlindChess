Quick Rules

  • Board is 4×8. A turn is either flip one hidden piece or move one revealed piece.
  • Most pieces move/capture 1 square orthogonally (no diagonals).
  • Cannon (砲/炮) captures by jumping exactly one piece in a straight line.
  • Capture order (high → low): General, Advisor, Elephant, Chariot, Horse, Cannon, Soldier.
  • Special case: Soldier can capture General; General cannot capture Soldier.
  • You win by capturing all opponent pieces.
